Find hospitals and oncologists from a prompt

Describe what you need in your own words. We find official hospital websites, read their doctor pages, and keep only what the pages publish. Directory portals (Practo, Justdial…) are never used as a source.

Run limits and options

Crawl each site proves a hospital by reading its own pages: it is listed only when the extractor actually reads doctors from it, and it needs no AI model. AI judges each page uses the configured model instead, and is the one to pick for conditions about individual doctors (email required, experience, language). These are work limits, not promises of complete coverage. Shortfalls are reported.
